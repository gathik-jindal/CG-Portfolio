Project A3 · Raw WebGL 2

WebGL Shader Renderer

A from-scratch WebGL renderer with a custom PLY loader and hand-written GLSL lighting. You can switch between per-vertex and per-pixel shading and compare specular models on 50+ classic 3D models.

  • Gouraud (per-vertex) vs Phong (per-pixel) shading
  • Standard Phong vs Blinn-Phong specular highlights
  • Two independent, toggleable light sources
  • Spherical & cylindrical UV texture mapping

Project A4 · Three.js + custom shaders

Physics Simulation Engine

A physics playground driven by a custom scene graph. A ball rolls down ramps, is struck by a swing arm on a rotating disc and knocks over a domino, all lit by custom Phong point lights and spotlights.

  • Hierarchical SGNode scene graph with animation callbacks
  • Gravity, ramps, and impulse-based collision response
  • Sphere–sphere, sphere–AABB and sphere–segment tests
  • Point light, side spotlight and a ball-tracking spotlight
Controls
